Synchronization

Extraction runs every 15 minutes on official quarter-hour boundaries (:00, :15, :30, :45). Failed extractions retry every minute until successful. Dashboard data refreshes every 5 minutes and also retries until successful.

Sync is incremental: the largest stored ID is read first, and only larger IDs are appended.

Sync Logic

  1. Read max(id) from Google Sheet.
  2. Open Shipments with status=rejected and ID descending.
  3. Extract only rows with IDs greater than the watermark.
  4. Stop when the previous ID watermark is reached and append safely to the Sheet.
  5. Record sync history and evaluate the alert threshold.
